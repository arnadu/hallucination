SOURCE PACKET: LUMA FIELD SYSTEMS
All names, organizations, products, and events are fictional.

1. Luma Field Systems was founded in 2018 by Priya Nandakumar
and Tomas Ekwueme. Nandakumar served as CEO from 2018 until 2020.

2. Daniel Ortiz was CEO from 2020 until March 15, 2023, when
Mira Chen succeeded him. Ortiz remains a member of Luma's
five-person board of directors.

3. Luma's headquarters is in Bellwick. Its only manufacturing
plant is in Eastmere. Luma also operates a repair depot in
Calder Ridge.

4. Luma launched its first product, the Sable Sensor, in 2021.
Its second product, the Tern Gateway, launched in 2023.

5. The Sable Sensor measures soil moisture and soil temperature.
It does not measure air humidity.

6. The Sable Sensor's published operating temperature range
is -10°C to 45°C, inclusive. The Tern Gateway's published
operating temperature range is -20°C to 50°C, inclusive.

7. The Sable Sensor uses two AA batteries. Its standard
package does not include batteries. The Tern Gateway is
mains-powered and ships with a power adapter.

8. The Sable Sensor's rated battery life is 9 months when it
takes one reading every 15 minutes.

9. Sable Sensors send their readings to a Tern Gateway over
Bluetooth. One Tern Gateway can connect to up to 20 Sable
Sensors at once.

10. Luma's 2025 annual report records revenue of 8.4 million
credits and operating expenses of 7.1 million credits. Its
2024 annual report records revenue of 6.2 million credits.

11. As of December 31, 2025, Luma employed 64 people:
40 in engineering, 14 in sales, and 10 in administration.
As of December 31, 2024, Luma employed 51 people.

12. Luma announced the Harbor Pilot on February 10, 2026.
The pilot is scheduled to begin on November 1, 2026,
and end on January 31, 2027.

13. The Harbor Pilot will involve 12 farms, all located within
80 km of Bellwick. Only farms within 80 km of Bellwick are
eligible. Participation is free. Participants must return the
loaned sensors within 14 calendar days after the pilot ends.

14. Luma's standard warranty lasts 24 months from purchase.
It covers manufacturing defects but excludes accidental
damage and damage caused by immersion in water. An extended
warranty of 12 additional months is available for the Tern
Gateway only.

15. Customers can request a refund within 30 calendar days
after delivery if the product is unused and in its
original packaging.

16. Luma sells directly to customers and does not use
resellers or distributors.

17. Luma's support desk is open Monday through Friday,
09:00–17:00 Bellwick local time. It is closed on
Bellwick public holidays.

18. Firmware versions 2.3 and 2.4 apply to the Sable Sensor.
Version 2.3 added CSV export. Version 2.4 fixed a Bluetooth
reconnection bug. The Tern Gateway runs a separate firmware
line. Firmware version 2.5 for the Sable Sensor is planned
for release in the first quarter of 2027.
